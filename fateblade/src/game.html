HOW TO PLAY

Reduce the enemy Sovereign to 0 HP. Play cards with mana (ramps +1 per turn, max 10), attack with your minions once each per turn. Your deck is 10 warlords × 2 copies, led by a Sovereign.

SOVEREIGN

Each deck is led by one of its realm's Legendary cards. The Sovereign sits in the hero box and grants a passive ability all game — printed under their portrait (hover for the full text).

REALM IDENTITY

Every realm has a gameplay identity (its own "color") — the Chaguan Horde charges, the Frosthold Dominion walls, the Ionaran Empire out-draws. Hover a realm on the pick screen to read its doctrine.

COIN FLIP

Each match flips a coin for who acts first. The second player draws an extra card; the first player's Sovereign gets First Rites — passive ×2, +1 mana, and your Realm Command costs (1) less on your first three turns.

FATIGUE

Drawing from an empty deck deals escalating damage to your hero (1, 2, 3…). Long games end — Guard walls cannot save you.

BATTLECRY

Does something the moment the card is played (damage, heal, draw, buff, or summon a Conscript).

DEATHRATTLE

Does something when the minion dies.

EDICT

A one-shot command — it resolves the moment it's played and is gone. Edicts ignore the 6-minion board limit.

RELIC

A persistent seat of power that sits beside your Sovereign all game (shown as gold chips above the hero). Max 2 relics per deck and 2 relic seats per side.

GUARD

Enemies must attack this minion before any other target.

CHARGE

This minion can attack the turn it is played.

LIFESTEAL

Damage this minion deals also heals your hero.

RANGED

This minion attacks without taking retaliation damage.

Realm Command (hero power, 2 mana, once/turn): your realm's own command — each of the fifteen realms fields a different one. Hover the power button for its text. Board holds 6 minions; hand holds 8 cards.
