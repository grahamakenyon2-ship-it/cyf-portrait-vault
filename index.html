What's In The Box

Full Deckbuilding

Sixteen realms, each with a unique Sovereign, card pool, and playstyle. Build decks from any pool and take them against the AI or a friend.

Two Campaigns

Two 8-chapter story campaigns — The Ironroad and The Broken Oath — with per-chapter rule twists, boss duels, and progression.

LAN Multiplayer

Duel a friend over your local network: shared lobby, live opponent status, turn timers, spectator mode, in-match chat, reconnect, and replay export.

Serious AI

Multiple difficulty tiers of computer opponents, trained and validated through tens of millions of simulated matches.

How It's Made

Fateblade is a one-person project, built with an AI-assisted development workflow — and a heavy bias toward measuring instead of guessing.

  • Every balance change is validated across millions of simulated AI matches, tracking win rates per realm before it ships
  • A card-economy model — benchmarked against published data from Magic: The Gathering, Hearthstone, and Gwent — prices every card against a measured stat curve
  • Nightly automated playtests run real AI agents through the game and file their own bug reports
  • Every release is verified with automated test suites and distributed through a checksum-verified patcher

Card data and balance reports are versioned and auditable. 370+ releases shipped.
